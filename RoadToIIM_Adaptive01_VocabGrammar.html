HOW THIS TEST WORKS
  • The first question is of medium difficulty. Every answer decides the next one: get it right and the next question gets harder; get it wrong and it gets easier.
  • You cannot go back. Each answer is locked once you confirm it.
  • Skip only if you truly don't know. A wrong guess pulls your level down more than a skip does.
  • Your result is a level from 1 to 5, not a raw score. Two students can both get 12 right and still reach different levels.
  • Grammar follows formal British exam conventions.
