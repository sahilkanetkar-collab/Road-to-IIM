04 · Getting in

Admission tests

Two different written tests, then an assessment and interview stage. Christ's pages say only "entrance test, assessment, interview"; the labels skill assessment and micro-presentation come from coaching sites and past candidates. Christ has not published the full 2027 calendar; past cycles ran later phases (around April and May) for students who miss Phase 1, with fewer seats left each time.

After the written test

  • Assessment and interview (described by coaching sites as skill assessment, micro-presentation and PI): only students with strong test scores are called. Christ does not publish the format or marks.
  • Prep focus: a short talk on a general topic, and clear answers to "why this programme" and "why Christ".
  • Money: first-year fee is due within 7 days of the result, often before IPMAT results.

06 · Prompts

Questions to ask before deciding

These bring out the things the sliders cannot capture.

  • Is Christ the main plan or the backup for IPMAT? Christ's first-year fee falls due within 7 days of the result, often before IIM results.
  • What did you enjoy most in Class 11–12: solving numericals, writing, debating, organising events, or building something?
  • Have you ever written code? Did you like it when it broke and you had to fix it?
  • Do you want a job at 21, or are you fine studying till 22–23 for a master's?
  • Is there a family business or a business idea you want to run?
  • What can the family spend in total, including Bangalore hostel and living costs?
  • Would you rather prepare for CAT later and do an MBA elsewhere, or lock in an MBA now?
  • Do you want to study abroad at some point? The Integrated MBA builds in an international immersion.
  • Which campus can you live with? Half of these options are only at Central campus. Pune Lavasa is closer to Ahmedabad and cheaper, and its page calls the BBA fully residential (confirm, since the same page also says day scholar), but it is remote.
  • Have you checked the cancellation and refund rules on the offer letter?
  • For the Integrated MBA: what happens if you want to leave after 3 years? Christ's page does not say whether you exit with a BBA. Ask admissions in writing before paying.
  • For any 4-year BBA: is the 4th year worth the extra fee for you, or will you exit after 3 and start working or preparing for CAT?

Sources and caveats

No BBA in Entrepreneurship appears in Christ's 2027 programme lists. Its dedicated entrepreneurship degree is the B.Com (Branding and Entrepreneurship) at Central campus, which is the one covered here. Every BBA also carries Entrepreneurship Development as a core course in Semester 4.
The strength matcher's weights are a counsellor's judgement, not research data. Tune them in the matcher panel as you learn what works.
Placement figures for Christ BBAs are thin. Christ publishes MBA placement reports, not programme-wise BBA ones. Third-party sites quote university-wide averages around ₹6.5–7 LPA, which mix all programmes. Fees "may rise by a nominal amount" each year, per Christ.
